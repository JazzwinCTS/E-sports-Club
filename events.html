Cookies and storage on this site

We use cookies and other tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience on our website. Here is everything we store, and where:

  • One cookie, named “returningVisitor”, so we can welcome you back instead of showing you this again. It lasts 30 days
  • Your settings — theme, filters, favourite team, member account and tickets. These are not cookies; they are kept in your browser's local and session storage, and the session ones disappear when you close the tab
  • Our social buttons — the X, Facebook, Discord and Instagram widgets in the footer load from those companies, which may set cookies of their own
