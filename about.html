Who we are

A community, not a tryout

NextGen E-Sports Club is a community for students, casual gamers and competitive players who share an interest in esports.

Our community focuses on three games: VALORANT, Counter-Strike 2 and PUBG. Members can take part in casual gaming sessions, workshops, team activities and organised competitions.

Competitive experience is welcome, but players do not need to be highly ranked to become part of the community.
