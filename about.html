Who we are

A community, not a tryout

NextGen E-Sports started with a handful of students booking the same lab every Thursday just to play together. It has grown into a relaxed community around Valorant, Counter-Strike and PUBG — open to amateur players, students, and anyone who just wants somewhere fun to play.

You do not need to be good to belong here. Most of what the team does is casual — scrim nights, LAN socials and the occasional workshop. We also host the 2026 Youth Championship for 16 to 18-year-olds, but that is one competition we run, not a bar to clear before you can join.
