# Youth Championship — Tournament Brief

## Purpose

The tournament page brings the same eight fictional organisations into three separate youth divisions while sharing one venue and one broadcast channel. The programme avoids overlaps by assigning PUBG to Friday, Counter-Strike 2 to Saturday and VALORANT to Sunday.

## Competition plan

- **Friday — PUBG:** five 45-minute lobby matches from 6:00 PM for three weeks; fifteen rounds use cumulative placement and elimination points.
- **Saturday — Counter-Strike 2:** BO3 matches run at 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM (three-hour broadcast windows); the 1:00 PM Grand Final is a standalone BO5.
- **Sunday — VALORANT:** BO3 matches run at 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM (two-and-a-half-hour broadcast windows); the 1:00 PM Grand Final is a standalone BO5.
- One Malaysia-time clock controls every page state. A fixture moves from UPCOMING to LIVE at its scheduled start, then to COMPLETED after its broadcast window. The same transition updates the score, winner artwork, next bracket participant and countdown.
- The local tournament trailer loops before play. During a live window the player changes to the embedded YouTube broadcast, with a red-dot LIVE badge and a direct YouTube viewing option.
- Weekly MVP reveals follow each division's final weekly broadcast: Friday 11:00 PM for PUBG, Saturday 7:30 PM for CS2 and Sunday 7:00 PM for VALORANT. The shorter standalone VALORANT Grand Final week reveals at 6:00 PM, one hour after its expected finish.

## Scope boundary

This page contains tournament fixtures only. General social events, workshops and community activities remain the responsibility of the separate Events page. Rankings are not changed by this redesign.
