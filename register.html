What happens next

  1. Your account is created the moment the form validates.
  2. A committee member reviews it and says hello.
  3. You are invited to the next social or scrim night to meet everyone.
  4. Want a more competitive scene? Optional tryouts run periodically.
  5. Otherwise, you are straight into the community — nobody is turned away.
